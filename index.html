HealthTech Mobile & Web Platform Healthcare Operations

Shift App

Healthcare workforce & shift management system designed for Nigerian nurses and hospital managers.

Shift App tackles the chaotic, manual shift scheduling common in Nigerian healthcare centers. It provides nurses with an effortless mobile interface to view rosters, swap shifts smoothly, and receive coverage alerts, alongside a comprehensive web manager dashboard for hospital administrators to track real-time ward coverage and prevent nurse burnout.

Nurse Mobile App: Clean calendar roster, 1-tap shift swaps & request off.
Manager Operations Dashboard: Ward coverage overview, schedule builder & on-call tracker.
Reliable Local Design: Optimized for quick glances between hospital rounds.

02
FinTech Digital Banking Mobile Product

Jun Bank

An easy, modern digital bank designed around the daily habits of an average Nigerian.

Nigerian digital banking often suffers from confusing financial menus, delayed status confirmations, and clumsy transfer pathways. Jun Bank strips away friction with a clean, high-contrast interface: fast Naira transfers in 2 taps, instant virtual debit card issuance, categorized spending charts, and an architecture built to function flawlessly even on unstable mobile data.

Zero-Friction Money Transfers: Instant recipient search, Naira balance display & quick repeats.
Virtual Card Management: Freeze/unfreeze cards on demand with clear security controls.
Stress-Free Receipts: Beautiful transaction cards with shareable payment proofs.

03
AI & FoodTech Social Delivery Logistics UX

Jun Foods

AI-powered food delivery designed for everyday Nigerians to eliminate decision fatigue.

Ordering food shouldn't take 30 minutes of indecision. Jun Foods utilizes smart AI taste profiling to immediately suggest meals tailored to individual cravings (from smoky jollof and grilled suya to local soups). It also introduces a social "Plan Trips & Meals" mode for friends to pool their carts and vote on dishes, coupled with live GPS rider tracking on local landmark routes.

AI Taste Recommendations: Curates dishes matching user cravings and dietary likes.
Group & Trip Meal Planning: Synchronized carts and voting for friends ordering together.
Live Order & Courier Tracking: Transparent step-by-step progress from kitchen to doorstep.
